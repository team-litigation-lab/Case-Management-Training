#### 2 · Slide Body — Tell Them

*Why:* The scheduling order entered 12/10/2026 now runs John Doe's case to a 09/13/2027 trial calendar, and every date in it is absolute. A trial-prep checklist turns those dates into owned tasks; the KPIs prove, week by week, that each task is ahead of its deadline rather than behind it.

1. The scheduling order controls: pin it to the front of the file, 30-, 14- and 7-day alerts on every date. Our experts 03/15/2027, theirs 04/15/2027, mediation by 03/31/2027, discovery closes 05/28/2027, pretrial 08/12/2027, trial calendar 09/13/2027.
2. Every extension is in writing: once Atty. Thone agrees, draft the same-day written confirmation for his signature (“Per our phone call, we agree to extend … to [date]”); it goes out in his name, never the CM's. A scheduling-order date usually moves only by court order.
3. Trap 1: treating court deadlines like adjuster deadlines. A missed disclosure can keep a causation opinion out of trial.
4. Trap 2: letting the client talk to the other side. Once the firm represents John Doe, and above all in suit, everything from the other side goes through counsel: if Keystone or a defense investigator reaches John Doe, step in, end it and tell Atty. Thone that day.
5. For John Doe's deposition (02/18/2027, 10:00 a.m., videoconference): confirm Riverton Court Reporting, send the link, run a technology check, align three calendars, and send the attorney-approved prep packet a week out, by 02/11/2027.
6. Prepare the process, never the testimony. The packet carries the ground rules (listen, pause, never guess, tell the truth); what John Doe says is for Atty. Thone's prep sessions.
7. Once suit forces the liability proof, defenses often pivot to causation and damages; here the 14-Day Gap is the trap. Assemble the gap record Atty. Thone preps from: EMS report, attendance record, texts, receipt, MRI read, causation letter.
8. Run final records from every provider before expert disclosure and again before pretrial; never copy the pre-lit package.
9. Draft trial subpoenas for the witnesses Atty. Thone names; build indexed, tabbed binders from the litigation file.
10. Write every KPI as a formula with a target, read it from the log, and audit the docket weekly. A miss is recorded and routed, never smoothed over.

*Nine litigation KPIs in three families, each with a target you can measure*

1. **Pleadings & service** — Service Check: a server assigned within 48 hours of filing. 14-Day Service Milestone: unserved at day 14 (10/08/2026) → escalate to Atty. Thone for skip-trace authority, run it on his approval, status update to him.

### 5.2.3 Diminished Value & PD Dispute Resolution

> **Lesson** · Day 5 — Litigation — Track B · Module 2: Interactive Property Damage Practice Lab  
> **Topic:** What a repaired car still loses, and how the dispute gets resolved  
> **Continuity:** John Doe v. Robert Smith · Atty. Elijah Thone · $10,000 BI / $20,000 HOST UM / $10,000 REL UM · 14-Day Gap  
> **Theory extracted from:** `D5#35` Property Damage: Calculating the Value of the Claim · `D5#34` Property Damage in Litigation: Types of Damages You Can Recover · LSH Claims Specialist: liability disputes — written basis, stronger evidence, escalation to the attorney · Newly authored: diminished value, the repair-plus-residual-loss measure (Restatement (Second) of Torts § 928 as an example), the 17c formula and its critique, the PD-only release

#### 1 · Visual Layout Description

A balanced two-column face-off. Left, a navy card headed KEYSTONE'S 17c: the formula as a descending staircase of three mono pills, $1,760.00 → × 0.75 → × 0.60, landing on $792.00, each step tagged in red with its flaw (10% CAP · TIER · MILEAGE TWICE). Right, an orange-bordered cream card headed THE MARKET: $3,600.00 set large in Fraunces, with three evidence chips beneath it (appraisal 05/06/2026 · A-pillar supplement · photos). A thin horizontal rule between the cards marks the $1,760.00 ceiling; the $3,600.00 sits visibly above it. Bottom strip, red-bordered: “PD release only. Never all claims.” The argument: the formula cannot reach the market.

#### 2 · Slide Body — Tell Them

*Why:* A Civic with an A-pillar repair in its history is worth less than one never hit, and John Doe didn't cause that loss. Keystone's $792.00 comes from a formula; the $3,600.00 from a market appraisal. The difference is pleaded on Track B: the CM builds the evidence and Atty. Thone makes the argument.

1. Diminished value is the market value a car still loses after a proper repair: buyers pay less for a damage history. Many jurisdictions allow it against the at-fault driver.
2. It sits beside the lesser-of rule: where a repair leaves a residual loss, many courts add it to the repair cost (for example, Restatement (Second) of Torts § 928), kept under the pre-loss value: $9,255.00 + $3,600.00 = $12,855.00, under $17,600.00. Confirm the governing rule.
3. It is a market loss, not a feeling. The measure is fair market value. Where an item has no market (an heirloom), some courts use actual value to the owner, still excluding sentimental value; a few allow emotional-distress damages for intentional destruction. Neither fits a 2019 Civic.
4. Keystone's “17c” figure is a carrier formula from a Georgia class-action settlement, not a legal measure of damages: 10% of $17,600.00 = $1,760.00, × damage 0.75, × mileage 0.60 = $792.00.
5. Critique the cap: no damage, however severe, yields more than $1,760.00 on this car. The appraisal's $3,600.00 is 20.5% of ACV, more than twice the formula's ceiling.
6. Critique the multipliers: 0.75 is the formula's ‘major structural’ tier; the A-pillar teardown supports arguing for its top, ‘severe structural’ tier (1.00), Atty. Thone's argument to make; 0.60 discounts 52,300 miles the $17,600.00 ACV already priced in, counting mileage twice.
7. Test your own evidence too: the Riverton Auto Appraisal (05/06/2026) post-dates the 04/24/2026 repair, so it valued the repaired car; check its method and credentials.
8. Resolve on evidence: get Keystone's position and inputs in writing, by the route Atty. Thone chooses (in suit, usually discovery), assemble the answer (appraisal, supplement, rental record) and escalate to him. Pleaded on Track B, the items resolve where he takes them: negotiation, mediation, or trial.
9. PD is settled only on a property-damage-only release Atty. Thone has reviewed. A release reaching “all claims” from 02/06/2026 could end the injury case in suit; John Doe signs nothing from Keystone until the firm has reviewed it.

> ⚠️ **UPL boundary.** You may calculate, critique the formula on paper, assemble the evidence and recommend a range to Atty. Thone. You may not make, accept, reject or counter any PD figure, tell John Doe what his car has lost in value or whether to take Keystone's offer, or explain a release to him.

> **Continuity:** John Doe v. Robert Smith · Atty. Elijah Thone · $10,000 BI / $20,000 HOST UM / $10,000 REL UM · 14-Day Gap  
> **Theory extracted from:** `D5#10` Discovery Intake Meeting — Pillar 2: The Essential Testimony Guidelines · `D5#12` Navigating the Silent Traps — Drill A: The “Is That All?” Trap · `D5#13` Navigating the Silent Traps — Drill B: The Thoughtful Silence · `D5#15` What Will the Defense Attorney Ask Me? #### 1 · Visual Layout Description

A wide trap map in three columns. Left: seven red-bordered trap cards stacked vertically, each with a mono label — IS THAT ALL? · COMPOUND · “DEGENERATIVE” · GAP MISCHARACTERISED · SEATBELT · 2021 PRIOR · SILENT STARE. Centre: seven navy rule chips with orange numbered badges ①–⑦ (TRUTH · PAUSE · SAY IT · NO GUESSING · SHORT · SILENCE · DON'T ARGUE), each trap joined to its rule by a thin orange leader. Footer strip in red: OBJECTION → STOP TALKING.

#### 2 · Slide Body — Tell Them

*Why:* Atty. Mercer doesn't need John to lie: a hurried “yes” to a narrowed list, a guess or an argument does the same damage. His Answer and the records show where he will dig: the gap, the disruption, the 2021 visits, the seatbelt. Seven general rules and Atty. Thone's objections meet every trap; a script meets none.

1. Defense questions fall into four categories: personal background (work, homes, earlier lawsuits), the crash mechanics, medical history (this injury and anything before it), daily life. Clients underreport the third most; here, the 2021 low-back visits.
2. Rule 1, the absolute truth. A damaged case can be saved; a lied-about one cannot. The prior-injury question is the Liar's Trap: counsel usually already has the records and is testing honesty.
3. Rules 2 and 3: pause two full seconds (time to think, a clean transcript, a window for Atty. Thone to object; on video it also absorbs the lag), then answer in words. The reporter cannot type a nod or “uh-huh.”
4. Rules 4 and 5: never guess or speculate. An honest estimate is fine; a guess dressed as fact is not, and “I don't know” or “I don't recall” is a complete answer. Answer only what was asked, then stop: the smallest target.
5. Rules 6 and 7: after a short answer, hold the silence and wait for the next question, eyes on the camera. Don't try to win: a deposition is information gathering, and the legal battle is Atty. Thone's.
6. The “Is that all?” trap: “So it was just the neck stiffness. Is that all?” A flat “yes” to a list from memory can later fence off the hand numbness and arm symptoms. Atty. Thone drills it: the complete medical record, not memory, is the full list.
7. Compound and loaded questions fold two facts, or a false premise, into one “yes”: “You waited three weeks because you felt fine?” General rule: if a question has two parts or is unclear, say so. The record says 14 days.
8. Objections: “Objection, form” is a routine flag on the record. John stops and lets the objection finish. Then he answers the question unless Atty. Thone instructs him not to; often he will hear “You can answer.”
9. The gap and the seatbelt are not won in the deposition. The EMS report, the MRI's “no significant degenerative change at the adjacent levels”, Dr. Reyes's causation letter and the EDR are Atty. Thone's to use: in his set, never a script for John.

> ⚠️ **UPL boundary.** You may explain the seven rules, what an objection is and how the day runs.
