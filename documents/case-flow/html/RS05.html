Contingent Fee Retainer Agreement
Personal injury · motor vehicle collision

This Agreement is made on February 9, 2026 between John Doe, 2214 Maple Hollow Rd, Riverton ("Client"), and LSH Law Group, 500 Commerce St, Suite 1200, Riverton ("the Firm").

1. Scope of representation

Client retains the Firm to pursue all claims for Client's personal injuries and related losses arising from the motor vehicle collision of February 6, 2026 at Maple Hollow Rd and Birch Ave, Riverton, in which Client was a passenger, against Robert Smith, any other responsible person, and any insurer that may owe benefits, including liability, uninsured/underinsured motorist and medical payments coverage. The Firm will also assist with Client's property-damage claim for his 2019 Honda Civic LX. This Agreement does not cover an appeal, workers' compensation, criminal or traffic matters, or any claim against Client; those require a separate written agreement.

2. Attorney's fee

The Firm is paid only if there is a recovery. The fee is a percentage of the gross recovery — the total of all money recovered by settlement, judgment, award or otherwise, before deduction of costs, liens or medical bills:

Stage at which the recovery is obtainedFee
Before a lawsuit is filed33⅓%
Once a lawsuit has been filed (through trial or arbitration)40%

The fee tier is set by whether a lawsuit has been filed at the time of the recovery, not by when the claim was first presented. The Firm will not file a lawsuit without first discussing it, and the change in fee, with Client. Recoveries from different insurers on the same collision are each subject to the fee tier in effect when that recovery is obtained. No fee is charged on property-damage payments made directly to a repair facility, towing company or rental company.

3. Case costs

The Firm will advance the reasonable costs of the claim, such as police and medical records, filing and service fees, court reporters, expert fees, certified mail, postage and copying. Costs are reimbursed to the Firm from Client's share after the fee is calculated; the fee is never calculated on the net. If there is no recovery, Client owes no costs. The Firm will obtain Client's approval before incurring any single cost over $1,500.00.

4. Medical bills, liens and reimbursement claims

Client authorizes the Firm to notify health plans, medical-payments carriers and providers of the claim; to negotiate any lien, subrogation or reimbursement claim; and to pay from the recovery, before disbursing Client's share, every lien or claim the Firm is legally or ethically required to honor, including letters of protection the Firm issues at Client's request. Client remains responsible for his medical bills. No lien will be compromised and no settlement disbursed without a written settlement statement signed by Client.

5. Settlement authority

The decision to settle belongs to Client. The Firm will not accept or reject any offer without Client's authority, and will communicate every offer to Client. Client agrees not to settle or sign any release, check or document from an insurer without the Firm's review, and understands that a release of one party can affect claims against others, including uninsured/underinsured motorist claims.

6. Client's responsibilities

  1. Not to speak with, give a statement to, or sign anything for any insurance company or other party about the collision, and to refer every such contact to the Firm.
  2. To keep all medical appointments, follow medical advice, and tell the Firm promptly of any missed appointment and the reason.
  3. To keep the Firm informed at all times of any change of address, telephone number or email, and to respond to the Firm's calls, texts and letters within a reasonable time.
  4. To tell the truth about the collision, prior injuries, prior claims and medical history, and to preserve evidence, including photographs, video, receipts and messages.
  5. Not to post about the collision, injuries or physical activities on social media, and not to delete anything already posted.

7. Loss of contact; withdrawal

If Client cannot be reached, the Firm will attempt contact by telephone, text, email and letter to the last known address, and through Client's emergency contact, and will document each attempt. If Client remains unreachable after documented attempts over at least thirty (30) days, or fails to cooperate as required by section 6, the Firm may withdraw by written notice to Client's last known address, subject to court approval where a lawsuit is pending. On withdrawal the Firm will take reasonable steps to protect Client's claims, including notice of any approaching deadline.

8. Termination

Client may end this Agreement at any time by written notice. If Client does so, the Firm may claim a reasonable fee for work performed, payable only from any later recovery, and reimbursement of costs advanced. The Firm may end this Agreement for good cause as permitted by the rules of professional conduct.

9. No guarantee

The Firm has made no promise or guarantee about the outcome of the claim, the amount of any recovery, or how long it will take. Any statement about possible results is an opinion only.

10. Communications and authorization

Client authorizes the Firm to communicate with Client by mobile phone, text message and email at the numbers and addresses on file, and to discuss the claim with Client's mother, Margaret Doe, as emergency contact, limited to locating Client and scheduling.

11. Entire agreement

This is the entire agreement between Client and the Firm about the fee. Any change must be in writing and signed by both. Client acknowledges that he has read this Agreement, has had the opportunity to ask questions, and has received a signed copy.
