3. Passenger status — why it matters

The client was the front-seat passenger in a vehicle he does not own. That status is what reaches both UM layers: Host UM as an occupant of the Escape, and Relative UM as a resident relative of Margaret Doe occupying a non-owned vehicle. The client owns no vehicle and has no auto policy of his own. The parked Civic is Margaret's, insured by Bluewater (BWA-209-66184); she recalls signing something about UM in 2024 — get the dec page and any signed rejection form, because UM on her Bluewater policy would add a resident-relative layer. A file that lists him as a driver points at the wrong coverage. Every intake field, letter and pleading must show him as a passenger.
