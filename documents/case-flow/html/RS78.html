At intake Mr. Doe described panic symptoms as a car passenger, avoidance of car travel and of the intersection at Maple Hollow Rd and Birch Ave, intrusive memories and dreams of the collision, poor sleep, low mood and withdrawal from friends and recreation. He reported no anxiety, depression or counseling before the collision of 02/06/2026 and no thoughts of self-harm at any session. Clinical impression: symptoms consistent with an adjustment disorder with mixed anxiety and depressed mood (F43.23), which he relates, and his history supports, to the collision of 02/06/2026, with a marked worsening after the episode of 04/02/2026.

Treatment and progress

Cognitive-behavioral work: breathing and grounding skills, a graded plan for car travel (short daytime rides with his mother, then evening rides to therapy, then the intersection itself), and sleep routines. He attended every session. By 06/05/2026 he rode as a passenger to all his appointments, had driven past the corner with his mother twice, and was sleeping better. He still reports tension as a passenger at intersections and avoids the front of his house when he can.

Recommendations

Continue the skills practiced. If symptoms return or worsen, further counseling through his health plan is appropriate. I have not provided, and do not provide, any opinion beyond the counseling described here.
