4. What the acknowledgment establishes, and what it does not

  • It fixes the start date from the carrier's own writing. Keystone states that it received the certified mailing on 07/22/2026 and that the package is complete at 82 pages, Exhibits A through K. That matches the signed return receipt. The thirty-day window therefore closes 08/21/2026, docketed with reminders at 14 and 7 days and on the day.
  • It closes the "we never got all of it" argument. The adjuster confirmed the page count and the exhibit range, so a later claim that an exhibit was missing is answered by her own email.
  • It is not an extension, and it is not an answer on the number. "I may come back to you about the time frame" is a request flagged, not a request granted. Only Atty. Thorne can move the date or the terms.
  • The Case Manager gave nothing away. No valuation, no view of the offer, no agreement to anything: the email asked for dates and a page count and pointed every question about terms to the attorney.
