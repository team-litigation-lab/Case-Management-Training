Mediated Settlement Term Sheet
John Doe v. Robert Smith · State Court of Riverton County · Civil Action No. 26-CV-04417
Mediation session of Thursday, March 18, 2027

The undersigned parties, their counsel and the insurer for Defendant, having participated in court-ordered mediation before Hon. Carla Benton (Ret.), agree to settle the claims described below on the following terms. This term sheet is a binding and enforceable agreement; the parties will sign formal documents consistent with it, but its enforceability does not depend on them.

  1. Payment. Keystone Mutual Insurance, on behalf of Defendant Robert Smith, will pay $10,000.00 — the full per-person bodily injury limit of policy KMI-PA-7731045 (claim KM-26-440871) — by check payable to "LSH Law Group IOLTA Trust Account f/b/o John Doe", delivered to LSH Law Group within twenty-one (21) days after Keystone receives the signed release described in paragraph 2 and a Form W-9.
  2. Limited release. Plaintiff John Doe will sign a limited release releasing only Robert Smith and Keystone Mutual Insurance from claims for bodily injury arising from the collision of February 6, 2026. The release will not release, and will expressly preserve, all of Plaintiff's uninsured/underinsured motorist (UM/UIM) claims, including his claims under Harbor Point Insurance policy HPA-4402917 (claim HP-26-7741) and Cardinal State Mutual policy CSM-AU-5580213 (claim CS-26-20355), and will not release any other person, firm, corporation or insurer. The release will contain no confidentiality clause binding on Plaintiff as against his UM carriers or lienholders.
  3. Affidavit of no other insurance. With the payment, Robert Smith will deliver his sworn affidavit that, on February 6, 2026, he had no other liability insurance (including any umbrella or excess policy) and was not acting in the course of any employment or business.
  4. Dismissal. Within ten (10) days after the settlement funds clear, Plaintiff will file a stipulation dismissing with prejudice his claims against Robert Smith for bodily injury. Each side bears its own attorney's fees and costs.
  5. Property damage excluded. Plaintiff's property-damage claims (loss of use and diminished value of his 2019 Honda Civic; Keystone PD claim KM-26-440871-PD) are not part of this settlement and are not released. Counsel will address them separately.
  6. UM consents. Harbor Point Insurance and Cardinal State Mutual have each consented in writing to this settlement and waived subrogation against Robert Smith. Their written consents, received during the session, are attached as Exhibits A and B.
  7. Liens. Plaintiff and his counsel will satisfy from the settlement proceeds any valid lien or reimbursement claim of a health plan or medical provider arising from treatment paid for or rendered on account of the collision.
  8. Report to the Court. The mediator will report to the Court that the claims against Robert Smith have settled as stated here.
