Harbor Point Insurance · Form HP-UM-14 (rev. 01/2025)Claim HP-26-7741
Waiver of Subrogation
Uninsured/Underinsured Motorists Coverage · Policy HPA-4402917 · Claimant John Doe
Insurer: Harbor Point InsuranceDate of loss: 02/06/2026
Named insured: Kevin AlvarezClaimant: John Doe (occupant)
Tortfeasor: Robert SmithLiability insurer: Keystone Mutual Insurance, claim KM-26-440871

RECITALS. John Doe claims bodily injury from a collision on February 6, 2026, while an occupant of a 2020 Ford Escape insured by Harbor Point Insurance under policy HPA-4402917. Keystone Mutual Insurance, the liability insurer of Robert Smith, has tendered its bodily injury limit of $10,000.00 per person (tender of August 12, 2026), and Robert Smith has sworn that no other liability insurance covers him for the collision (affidavit of August 10, 2026). By letter of August 13, 2026, counsel for John Doe asked Harbor Point Insurance to consent to that settlement and to waive subrogation.

NOW, THEREFORE, Harbor Point Insurance, by its authorized representative, states and agrees:

  1. Waiver. Harbor Point Insurance waives, releases and will not assert any right of subrogation, reimbursement or recovery against Robert Smith or Keystone Mutual Insurance for any uninsured/underinsured motorists benefits it has paid or may pay to or for John Doe arising from the collision of February 6, 2026.
  2. Consent. Harbor Point Insurance consents to John Doe's settlement of his bodily injury claim against Robert Smith and Keystone Mutual Insurance for $10,000.00, and to his release of Robert Smith and Keystone Mutual Insurance only. It will not substitute its own payment for the amount tendered.
  3. No release of the UM claim. This waiver is not a release, settlement or adjudication of John Doe's claim for uninsured/underinsured motorists benefits under policy HPA-4402917, which remains open subject to the terms and conditions of the policy.
  4. Reliance. Harbor Point Insurance gives this waiver knowing that John Doe, Robert Smith and Keystone Mutual Insurance will rely on it in settling, and that it may be filed or offered in any court or arbitration proceeding concerning this claim.
  5. Authority. The undersigned is the claims representative assigned to claim HP-26-7741 and is authorized by Harbor Point Insurance to give this waiver on its behalf.
