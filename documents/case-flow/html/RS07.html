Narrative

On 02/06/2026 at approx. 0710 hrs I was dispatched to a two-vehicle crash with a parked vehicle struck at Maple Hollow Rd and Birch Ave, an all-way stop intersection. Arrived 0716. All stop signs present and unobstructed.
Unit 2 (Ford Escape, driver Alvarez) was eastbound on Maple Hollow Rd. D2 stated he stopped at the stop sign, saw no one at the other approaches, and proceeded. Unit 1 (Chevrolet Silverado, driver Smith) was northbound on Birch Ave. D1 stated he "thought the other car was going to wait" and did not recall whether he stopped. W1 (Calloway), on foot at the SW corner, stated Unit 2 came to a complete stop and Unit 1 did not slow down.
Unit 1 failed to stop at the stop sign for northbound traffic and struck Unit 2's front passenger door in the SE quadrant of the intersection. The impact pushed Unit 2 north into Unit 3 (Honda Civic, unoccupied), legally parked at the east curb of Birch Ave. No skid marks from Unit 1 located before the POI. Debris field in the NE quadrant.
P1 (Doe), front-right passenger of Unit 2, complained of neck pain; evaluated by RFD Medic 7 and refused transport. Residential doorbell camera at 2214 Maple Hollow Rd (NE corner) faces the intersection; owner advised to preserve the recording. D1 cited (below).
