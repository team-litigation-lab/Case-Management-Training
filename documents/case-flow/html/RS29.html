Dear Atty. Thorne:

Having reviewed the documents your office provided with the policy, Cardinal State Mutual confirms that John Doe is an insured for Uninsured/Underinsured Motorists Coverage under policy CSM-AU-5580213.

Resident relative. The policy defines a "family member" as a person related to the named insured by blood, marriage or adoption who is a resident of the named insured's household. Mr. Doe is Margaret Doe's son and resided in her household at 2214 Maple Hollow Rd on the date of loss.

Non-owned vehicle. On 02/06/2026 Mr. Doe was a front-seat passenger in a 2020 Ford Escape owned by Kevin Alvarez, a vehicle not owned by Mr. Doe or any member of the household, so the exclusion below does not apply.

Policy excerpt — Part C, Exclusions, A.1
We do not provide Uninsured/Underinsured Motorists Coverage for "bodily injury" sustained by any "insured" while "occupying", or when struck by, any motor vehicle owned by you or any "family member" which is not insured for this coverage under this policy.

Limit and order of payment. The Uninsured/Underinsured Motorists limit is $10,000.00 each person / $20,000.00 each accident. Because Mr. Doe was occupying a vehicle the named insured does not own, this coverage is excess over any other collectible uninsured/underinsured motorists coverage, here the coverage on the Harbor Point policy for the 2020 Ford Escape, which applies first.

Policy excerpt — Part C, Other Insurance, ¶ 2
Any insurance we provide with respect to a vehicle you do not own shall be excess over any other collectible insurance providing coverage on a primary basis.

Conditions. Payment under this coverage requires that the limits of all applicable bodily injury liability policies be exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements. Please give us written notice of any tentative settlement with the at-fault driver's insurer before any release is signed; Part C requires our written consent, and we will consent or advance payment of the tentative settlement amount within 30 days of the notice.

This position is based on the information now available, under a reservation of rights as to facts not yet known. Damages have not yet been evaluated.
