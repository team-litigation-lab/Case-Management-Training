Scheduling Order

This action came before the Court for Case Management following the filing of Defendant's Answer on November 12, 2026. Having reviewed the pleadings and the parties' proposed scheduling deadlines, and for good cause shown, the Court ORDERS that the following deadlines shall govern this action. These deadlines may be modified only by order of the Court upon motion showing good cause; an agreement of counsel alone does not extend any deadline set below.

No.EventDeadline
1Plaintiff's disclosure of expert witnesses, with the information required by the rules for each expertMonday, March 15, 2027
2Mediation completed (see paragraph 4)Wednesday, March 31, 2027
3Defendant's disclosure of expert witnessesThursday, April 15, 2027
4Close of discovery — all discovery, including expert depositions, completedFriday, May 28, 2027
5Pretrial conference, 9:00 a.m., Courtroom 4A, Riverton County CourthouseThursday, August 12, 2027
6Case placed on the jury trial calendarMonday, September 13, 2027

IT IS FURTHER ORDERED:

  1. Discovery. Written discovery shall be served so that responses are due on or before the close of discovery. Discovery requests served so late that responses would fall due after the close of discovery will not be enforced.
  2. Expert witnesses. A party who fails to disclose an expert witness by the deadline above may not call that witness at trial except upon leave of Court for good cause shown.
  3. Motions. Discovery disputes shall be brought to the Court only after counsel have conferred in good faith, and any motion shall so certify.
  4. Mediation. The parties shall mediate this action and complete mediation by March 31, 2027, as provided in the Court's separate Order of Referral to Mediation entered this date. Each party and each insurer with an interest in the outcome shall attend through a representative with full settlement authority.
  5. Pretrial order. Counsel shall confer and file a proposed consolidated pretrial order no later than seven (7) days before the pretrial conference. Lead counsel for each party shall attend the pretrial conference in person.
  6. Trial. The Court will issue a trial calendar listing the cases called for the term beginning September 13, 2027. Counsel shall be ready to proceed on that calendar.
  7. Settlement. Counsel shall notify the Court in writing within two (2) business days of any settlement.

SO ORDERED this 10th day of December, 2026.
