Plan provisions (excerpt)

Peach State Health Plan Group Policy GRP-41208, issued to Northgate Distribution — Certificate of Coverage, Section 11 (Third-Party Liability: Reimbursement and Subrogation).

11.1 The benefits described in this certificate are provided under a group insurance policy issued by Peach State Health Plan to the policyholder. The plan is fully insured; benefits are not paid from the assets of the policyholder.

11.2 If a covered person is injured by the act or omission of another person and Peach State Health Plan pays benefits for that injury, Peach State Health Plan is entitled to be reimbursed, to the extent of the benefits paid, from any amount the covered person recovers for the injury by settlement, judgment, award or otherwise, including any recovery from a liability insurer or from uninsured or underinsured motorist coverage.

11.3 The covered person must notify Peach State Health Plan of any claim against a third party, cooperate in the recovery, and do nothing to prejudice the plan's rights. Reimbursement is subject to applicable state law.
