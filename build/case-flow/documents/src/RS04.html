3. Passenger status — why it matters

The client was the front-seat passenger in a vehicle he does not own. That status is what reaches both UM layers: Host UM as an occupant of the Escape, and Relative UM as a resident relative of Margaret Doe occupying a non-owned vehicle. The client is unsure whether he kept UM on his own Bluewater policy (BWA-209-66184) and recalls signing something in 2024 — get the dec page and any signed rejection form. If UM was rejected and he had been driving his own Civic, he would reach only the $10,000 BI layer: Cardinal State's policy would likely exclude a resident relative occupying a vehicle he owns that it does not insure. Every intake field, letter and pleading must show him as a passenger.
