Order of Referral to Mediation

Pursuant to the Court's alternative dispute resolution program and its Scheduling Order entered this date, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

  1. Referral. This action is referred to mediation. Mediation shall be completed by March 31, 2027.
  2. Selection of mediator. Within thirty (30) days of this Order, counsel shall jointly select a registered mediator and file a notice identifying the mediator and the date of the session. If counsel cannot agree, the Court will appoint a mediator from its roster.
  3. Attendance. The following shall attend the mediation session in person unless the mediator, for good cause, permits attendance by videoconference: (a) each party; (b) lead trial counsel for each party; and (c) a representative of each insurer that may be obligated to pay all or part of any judgment, with full authority to settle without further consultation.
  4. Mediation statements. Each party shall deliver to the mediator, by the date the mediator sets, a confidential mediation statement, which shall not be filed with the Court.
  5. Confidentiality. All mediation communications are confidential and may not be used for any purpose in this action except as permitted by law. Nothing said or produced at mediation constitutes a waiver of any privilege.
  6. Fees. The mediator's fees shall be divided equally between the sides unless otherwise agreed, and paid as the mediator directs.
  7. Report. Within seven (7) days after the session, the mediator shall file a report stating only whether the case settled in whole or in part, or reached impasse. If the case settles, counsel shall submit the dismissal or other final papers promptly.
  8. Sanctions. Failure to comply with this Order may result in sanctions, including an award of the other side's fees and costs of the session.

SO ORDERED this 10th day of December, 2026.
