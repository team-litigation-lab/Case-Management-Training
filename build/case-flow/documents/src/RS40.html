Thank you for your notice that your firm represents the above member in connection with injuries sustained in a motor vehicle accident on February 6, 2026. Please accept this letter as Peach State Health Plan's notice of its reimbursement and subrogation rights.

The member is covered under a fully insured group health plan issued by Peach State Health Plan to Northgate Distribution, effective February 1, 2026. Under the terms of the group policy and certificate of coverage, when the Plan has paid or may pay benefits for an injury caused by a third party, the Plan is entitled to reimbursement from any recovery the member obtains for that injury, whether by settlement, judgment, award or otherwise, and from any source, including the responsible party's liability insurer and uninsured/underinsured motorist coverage.

Peach State Health Plan has paid or may pay claims for treatment related to this accident. The Plan hereby asserts its right of reimbursement for all such benefits from any recovery. An itemization of accident-related claims paid will be provided on request and before any settlement is finalized; the amount of the Plan's claim may change as additional claims are received and processed.

To allow us to protect the Plan's interest and avoid delay at settlement, please:

  1. Complete and return the enclosed Accident Questionnaire, or provide the same information by letter.
  2. Identify every insurance carrier involved (liability, medical payments, uninsured/underinsured motorist, and the member's own auto policy), with claim numbers and adjusters.
  3. Confirm your firm's representation and provide the name of the attorney responsible for the file.
  4. Provide a description of the accident and a copy of the police report when available.
  5. Notify this office in writing before any settlement is reached, and request a final lien amount before disbursement. Please do not disburse settlement proceeds without first resolving the Plan's claim.

Please also advise us of any change in representation. Thank you for your cooperation; please direct all correspondence about this recovery to me at the address above.
