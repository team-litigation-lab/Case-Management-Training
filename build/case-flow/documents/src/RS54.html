Definitions and instructions. "You" and "Plaintiff" mean John Doe. "Collision" means the motor vehicle collision of February 6, 2026, at the intersection of Maple Hollow Road and Birch Avenue, Riverton, described in the Complaint. Interrogatories

  1. State your full name and any other names you have used, your date of birth, the last four digits of your Social Security number, your driver's license number, and each address at which you have resided during the past ten (10) years with the dates of residence.
  2. Identify each person who answered or assisted in answering these Interrogatories, and each document consulted.
  3. Describe in detail how the Collision occurred, including where you were seated, the direction of travel of each vehicle, whether the vehicle in which you were riding stopped before entering the intersection, and what you observed immediately before and at the moment of impact.
  4. State the purpose of the trip during which the Collision occurred, its origin and destination, and describe your relationship with Kevin Alvarez, including any carpool or ride-sharing arrangement.
  5. State whether you were wearing a seatbelt at the moment of the Collision, and identify every person and document with knowledge of whether you were wearing a seatbelt.
  6. Identify every person who witnessed the Collision or its immediate aftermath, or who claims knowledge of the facts of the Collision or your injuries, and summarize the knowledge you believe each has.
  7. Identify every written, recorded or transcribed statement obtained from any person concerning the Collision, including any statement you gave to an insurer, and identify its custodian.
  8. Identify every photograph, video recording (including any doorbell, security or dash camera recording) or electronic data depicting or recording the Collision, the scene, the vehicles or your injuries, and identify its custodian.
  9. Describe each physical, mental or emotional injury you claim resulted from the Collision, and state whether you claim that any injury is permanent.
  10. Identify each health care provider who has examined or treated you since the Collision, and for each state the dates of treatment, the nature of the treatment and the amount charged.
  11. State each reason you did not seek medical treatment between February 6, 2026 and the date of your first medical treatment after the Collision, and describe everything you did to treat your symptoms during that period.
  12. Identify each health care provider who examined or treated you during the ten (10) years before the Collision, including any chiropractor, and for each state the dates and the condition treated.
  13. State whether, before the Collision, you ever experienced injury, pain or other complaints involving your neck, back, shoulders or arms, and if so, describe each such complaint, its cause and its treatment.
  14. Identify each scheduled medical, chiropractic or physical therapy appointment you missed, cancelled or rescheduled after the Collision, and state the reason for each.
  15. Itemize each element of special damages you claim, including medical expenses, prescription expenses, lost wages and property damage, stating for each the amount, the payee and whether it has been paid and by whom.
  16. Identify every source from which you have received or claimed payment for any damages claimed (including medical payments coverage, health insurance, uninsured motorist coverage and any other insurance), the amount received, and any lien, subrogation or reimbursement interest asserted.
  17. Identify each letter of protection, lien or other agreement under which any health care provider has deferred payment of its charges pending the outcome of your claim.
  18. Identify each employer for whom you have worked during the past ten (10) years, stating your position, dates of employment, rate of pay and reason for leaving.
  19. If you claim lost wages or lost earning capacity, state each date of work missed, your rate of pay, the method of calculation, and identify every document supporting the claim.
  20. Identify every motor vehicle collision, fall, sports injury, work injury, workers' compensation claim, insurance claim or lawsuit in which you were involved before or after the Collision.
  21. Identify each social media account, messaging application or online platform (including Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X and Snapchat) you have used since February 6, 2025, stating the user name for each.
  22. Identify each person (other than your attorneys) with whom you communicated by text message, email or social media about the Collision or your physical condition between February 6, 2026 and February 20, 2026.
  23. Identify every policy of insurance under which you have made or may make a claim for the injuries or losses alleged, including the policy number, the insurer, the coverage claimed and the status of the claim.
  24. Describe the damage to your 2019 Honda Civic, the repairs made, all amounts paid for repair, towing, storage and rental, and state the factual basis and amount of any claim for loss of use or diminished value.
  25. Identify each person you expect to call as an expert witness at trial, and for each state the subject matter, the substance of the facts and opinions to which the expert is expected to testify, and a summary of the grounds for each opinion.
