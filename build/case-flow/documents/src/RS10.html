Data limitations

Pre-crash data are recorded at 2 samples per second; values between samples are not recorded. Vehicle speed is the speed reported to the module by the vehicle's speed sensors and may differ from true ground speed during wheel slip, braking or lateral movement; reported speed accuracy is approximately ±1 mph. Time 0.0 is the module's algorithm-enable (event trigger) time. The module does not record calendar date or time of day; Event 1 is matched to the 02/06/2026 crash by the deployment record, the module's removal at repair in 03/2026 and the unchanged ignition-cycle count.
